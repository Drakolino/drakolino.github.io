HPS Order System, Phase II proposal

Problem

Make the case for a new order system and a safe way to roll it out.

Mechanism

Proposed an iterative build with a limited pilot, product-owner governance, demos every two weeks and UAT before pilot. Defined monitoring for failed payments, duplicate orders, inventory mismatches, response time and checkout completion.

Result

NPV of $63,665, ROI of 45.8% and breakeven in 2.61 years.
